Questions

The quick answers.

What's included in a house move?

Packing materials, disassembly and reassembly, loading, transport, unloading and placement — plus full insurance. You don't need to bring anything.

How long does a 2BHK move take?

A typical 2BHK inside Dubai takes 5–7 hours with a 3-person crew. Villas can be a full day with 4–6 crew.

Do you work on weekends?

Yes — Friday and Saturday are our busiest days. Book at least 3–5 days ahead during month-end peaks.

Can you provide a written quote?

Yes. Every quote is fixed, written, and VAT-inclusive. No surprises on moving day.
