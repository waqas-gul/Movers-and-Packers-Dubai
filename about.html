How we started

From a small Al Quoz warehouse to 10,000+ successful moves.

We started in 2014 in a single warehouse unit in Al Quoz Industrial Area — three founders, one box truck, and one rule: fixed quotes, no surprises on the day. Year one, we delivered 40 moves, mostly studios in Dubai Marina and one-bedrooms in JVC.

Word travelled. Happy customers told their friends, colleagues told their companies, and over time we earned the trust of full office floors, villa communities in Arabian Ranches and Emirates Hills, and thousands of families moving between the seven emirates.

Today we run 70+ moves a week with a permanent team of 50 uniformed staff, and a track record of 10,000+ completed moves. The standards have never changed — just the scale.
