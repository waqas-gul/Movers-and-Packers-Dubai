Questions

The quick answers.

Can you move just one item?

Yes — no minimum. A single sofa from AED 150, delivered anywhere in Dubai.

Do you assemble IKEA furniture?

Yes. PAX, MALM, BESTÅ, KALLAX, HEMNES — we do them daily. AED 150/hr for assembly-only bookings.

Will my items be wrapped?

Every piece is wrapped in moving blankets and bubble wrap. Fragile glass gets extra protection.

Can I book an hourly team instead?

Yes. Hourly teams (2 movers + truck) are AED 250/hr with a 2-hour minimum.
